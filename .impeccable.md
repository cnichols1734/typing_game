## Design Context

### Users
Desktop players who want an arcade run, not a typing exam. They sit at a keyboard, chase one more wave, and want a callsign on the board. Focused, slightly competitive, here for the loop.

### Brand Personality
Cinematic, metallic, orbital. Voice is clipped CIC telemetry — short, precise, no cheerleading. Emotion: tension held just under control, then a hard exhale when a streak breaks.

### Aesthetic Direction
Orbital defense at the edge of the system. Warm ink void tinted toward brass. Gunmetal hulls, scorched white panels, copper/amber fire, oxide-red threat. Nebulae in rust and low-chroma cobalt. Not lime-terminal 1995. Not cyan-neon AI space. Not a typing-test website with a game skin.

Typography: Tektur for titles, Archivo Narrow for telemetry, Atkinson Hyperlegible for in-world words (readability is sacred).

### Design Principles
1. Words are gameplay — never smear a letter with bloom or shake.
2. Every correct key is a physical event: flash, beam, spark, tick.
3. Spectacle on milestones; idle play stays readable.
4. Power-ups change the battlefield, not the keyboard.
5. Desktop-first. The keyboard is the controller.
